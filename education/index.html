Education

Harvard University
Bachelor of Science in Software Engineering
Class of 2024

Yes, I went to Harvard, and yes, it was as intense as it sounds. I graduated in 2024 with a degree in Software Engineering, and honestly it was one of the most challenging and rewarding things I have ever done. Late nights, tough problem sets, and more coffee than I would like to admit.

The degree gave me a solid grounding in the fundamentals, including algorithms, systems design, web technologies, and the kind of computer science theory that actually makes you think differently about problems. Beyond the technical side, Harvard taught me how to learn fast, think critically, and stay calm when things get complicated.

I walked away not just with a diploma, but with real confidence in my abilities and a genuine excitement for what is possible in software and web development. The hard work was worth it, and it is only the beginning.
